# Problem Statement and Justification

## 1. Introduction
For members of Maritime University Malaysia (UMT), a centralized Learning Management System (LMS) can provide a more organized and convenient way to manage academic activities. 

## 2. Problem Statement
Without an integrated platform, students may experience difficulties keeping track of course materials, assignment deadlines and academic information, while lecturers may face challenges in distributing learning materials, managing student submission and providing assessment feedback.

## 3. Justification
Therefore, the proposed Learning Management System (LMS) for Maritime University Members aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized online platforms for students, lecturers and administrators.
